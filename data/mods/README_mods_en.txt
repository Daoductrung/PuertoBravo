MODS FOR PUERTO BRAVO: LEY DEL ASFALTO

A mod is an expansion of the game: places, missions, vehicles, weapons or whole towns.
Each mod is a file in this folder, and it loads by itself when the game starts.

The game comes with nine: Bahía del Ocaso, Costa Dorada, Valdeolmos, Peñas Altas, Isla de los Vientos, Noches de neón,
Oficios y recados, Motor y asfalto, and El Diablo.

To add a mod someone has given you, copy its file here.
To remove one, delete it from here or turn it off in the game, in Options, Mods, or in the editor with Control M.
If a mod comes with its own sounds, they go in the sonidos folder inside this one.
To make your own, use EditorMods.exe, which is next to the game. Its manual opens with F1.
