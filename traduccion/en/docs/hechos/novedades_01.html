What's new in Puerto Bravo: Ley del Asfalto

What changes in each version, from the newest to the oldest.

Version 1.7.2

Made with what you told us in the forum thread and on GitHub after 1.7.1. Saved games load as before.

Fixed

  • The game no longer closes with an error when you press G to repeat the directions just as what you were looking for with the GPS had been reached: when you picked up an item, such as the money enemies drop, or when you got into your own vehicle that you had marked, including the one your mechanic had just brought you.
  • The game no longer closes suddenly, without saying anything, while it generates your own character's voices in the background. It happened on some computers with the downloaded game, for example when joining a server.
  • On a server, the vehicle your mechanic brings you from the garage is no longer lost. Before, it was not recorded as yours: Shift L and Services could not find it, it disappeared when you moved away, and it did not go back to the garage either. Now the server saves it as yours, just as if you had got out of it.
  • The vehicle they bring you no longer appears on top of another parked one, and with several vehicles close together, F picks yours, or the one the GPS is taking you to, instead of starting to force the lock of the one next to it.
  • The GPS autopilot no longer runs red lights with the vehicles that brake worse, like the bus or a truck: it brakes in time up to the line, and if the light is going to turn red before it crosses, it stops. In timed jobs, like the bus, the taxi or a delivery, it no longer runs any red light: before, it cost you fines, license points and the police on your tail.
  • With the GPS autopilot turned on in Options, when you get into a vehicle the game says whether it drives itself and, if not, why: because you are in free driving or because no destination is set. And Y says that on the water and in the air it does not take the controls.
  • When you type your character's name, or any text, the accented vowels from the accent key and the Windows input methods also work. With some keyboards, the box stayed empty.
  • On duty as a police officer, shooting or ramming the fleeing vehicle in a chase no longer costs you your badge for having gone too far: it is allowed up to three hundred meters. In the first chase, the game explains how to stop someone fleeing by car.
  • When you finish a qualification, the game says which permanent contract it is useful for and that it is signed at the Job center. Before, it said it opened a position, and some people went looking for a business to open.
  • When you open your game to your friends, the game tries a bit harder if the connection with the router drops halfway, instead of treating it as refused on the first try.
  • The server no longer fails to start if its configuration file was saved with an editor that does not use UTF-8, as happened on Linux after editing it or copying it from Windows: it reads it anyway and warns in the log that it is best to save it in UTF-8.
  • With realistic life, hygiene really counts. Before, being dirty only lowered your mood a little. Now, without showering, a date brings you less close, you do less well at work and you sell fewer cars; and if you smell bad, people trust you less, there are no kisses or nights together, and the club and the casino won't let you in. Being very hungry, very thirsty or dirty keeps lowering your mood.
  • The grenade and the other explosives now say grenades, cocktails or rockets, not bullets. They could already be thrown: you choose them with the comma or the I key and throw them with Space, or with Control at the wheel.
  • What you earn selling drugs is now dirty money, like the money from robberies.
  • The stock market now tells you what is happening. Before, prices moved, but nothing said so and it seemed that owning shares was useless. Now, every midnight, if you have shares, the game tells you what they are worth and how much you gain or lose; some companies pay dividends every week; there is news that makes a company go up or down; and the menu says the change since yesterday, what you paid and how much you gain or lose.
  • If you are arrested, the game now says everything they confiscate: besides firearms, the merchandise and the stolen goods you are carrying. And if you carry something like that, the officer who stops you frisks you: you can no longer pay the fine on the spot and keep the merchandise in your pocket.

New

  • Puerto Bravo in English. The whole game is translated: the menus, the story, the places, the items, the keys, the mod editor, the server panel and all the guides. When it opens, it checks the language of your Windows: Spanish if it is any variant of Spanish, and English otherwise. You can choose it in Options, on the first line, Language.
  • The story scenes also have recorded voices in English, each character with their own, with American and British accents. And if you play in English with a character you made yourself, the lines that say your name are generated with English voices.
  • The game can be translated into any language: each one is a folder in data, idiomas, named with the language code, and the game recognizes it by itself. The manual explains how, in the mods section. The guides go by language, like manual_es and manual_en, and the voices in one folder per story and language.
  • Your saved games work in any language: one saved in Spanish loads in English, and the other way around.
  • Components: languages, voices, guides, sound packs and mods can be published separately from the game, without waiting for a new version. They are in Options, Updates, Components, they are looked for in the same source as the versions and they install without closing the game. When it starts, the game tells you if there are new components.
  • Gangs and good people: if your standing with the neighbors reaches 40, a gang's members will often let you through their neighborhood with a scowl, and defending yourself from the ones who do attack you costs you nothing with their gang and does not start a neighborhood war. The police also come sooner to help you. And for everyone, knocking down in self-defense someone who attacked you first takes away much less reputation with their gang.
  • Renting out your houses: a house of yours that you don't use can be rented out from its menu. Every midnight you collect the rent, different each day, with some damage or a late payment now and then, and you get it back whenever you want by returning the deposit. Not on servers.
  • Your partner and your homes: if you have several houses, you choose which one you live in when you ask them, and you can move together to another one, also in another town. And you can give them money to buy themselves something: the next day they tell you what they bought. Sometimes they are the one who asks you.
  • Your people, by name: in Your gang, Enter on someone lets you talk to that person, invite them, give them a bonus or kick them out. What you give them makes them more loyal.
  • The message history: Control Page Up goes to the first one and Control Page Down to the last. And a new option, in Options, Voice, so that new messages don't pull you away from where you are reviewing.
  • The life of a boss. Your crew are paid according to the pay you choose, low, normal or high, and their loyalty goes up or down every day; if you forget about them they get angry, if you don't pay them the least loyal one leaves, and someone very fed up can betray you: rob you, go over to another gang or turn you in. You can give them a task: selling on the street the merchandise you keep at home, or cooking in your labs. At the end of the day they bring you the earnings.
  • Money, realistically: you pay with what you carry, so you have to withdraw it first at a bank or an ATM; what you carry can be taken from you in a mugging on the street; money can be kept at home, but a house can be burgled, unless it has a safe, and an alarm makes it harder.
  • Laundering: the bank no longer accepts dirty money just like that; above a daily limit, it notifies the tax office. It is laundered little by little at the bank, in casino chips, with a discreet fixer or with a front business.
  • Social life is lived in person: from the phone you call, text, make plans to meet, send a gift to their home, give money and break up; a date, a gift in hand, a kiss, the night, plans or asking someone out are done face to face, where that person is, in your home or while they are with you. A date is lived by going to the place together, and the plan is told in scenes. You can go back to doing everything from the phone, with the new option.
  • Meeting at a place and time: with the people in your social life and with your acquaintances from the street. The phone reminds you; if you get there, the plan is lived there; if not, you stood them up.
  • Schedules, optional: each person in your social life is at their place during their hours and has one day off a week; and, if you want, businesses open and close at their hours and close on some days. The first day of the game is Monday.
  • If your partner finds out there is someone else, the hurt lasts for days: what you do together brings you half as close, apologizing in person helps and, if it happens again, it's over. Kisses and dances with another person can also reach them.
  • More clothes, and with style: more than thirty outfits sorted by style, new footwear and many more accessories, such as bags, belts, bracelets, scarves, ties or perfumes. What you buy is yours: at home you can dress in it without paying. Each person in your social life likes a style, and dressing smartly helps you sell cars.
  • More ways to wash and to drink, with realistic life: the gym locker room shower, the sea, the toiletries section of the supermarket, the bazaar and the gas station, with deodorant, wipes, cologne and a toothbrush, and the fountains in the parks and squares, to drink for free. And in the shops, water jugs, sports drinks and canned coffee.
  • The backpack: you carry valuables, stolen or found, and the merchandise, with limited space. They turn up when you rob, in the glove compartment of the cars you break into, on some fallen enemies and at the bottom of the sea, and they are sold at pawn shops, stolen goods for less. Clothing stores sell a bigger backpack.
  • The wardrobe in your houses: it stores items, merchandise and weapons, safe from the police.
  • Insurance for your vehicles: you take it out on the phone, in My vehicles, and it covers all your land vehicles. Every midnight it charges a fee for each one you have on the street; the ones in the garage don't pay. If one explodes, the next day it is returned to you repaired and with its upgrades, in your garage or at the door of a repair shop, after paying an excess. Without insurance, the game tells you it has been lost.
  • Your businesses from a distance: when one with no employees has gone days without you stopping by, the warning now says that with someone hired it would hold up without you. With staff, its reputation stays a little below normal even if you don't go, so you can have businesses in several towns without visiting them all. The manual explains it.
  • Everything new can be adjusted in the mod editor, and can also be done with a controller.

Version 1.7.1

Made with what you told us and asked for after 1.7.0: the fixes, and also the big requests. What changes the way you play is optional, and at the end of this section it says how each option comes and where to change it. Games saved with earlier versions load as before.

The most important

  • Your vehicles are no longer lost: they stay where you park them, they have their own list on the phone and you can give them a name.
  • Defending yourself from someone who attacks you is no longer a crime, and neither are gym fights.
  • The gangs you defeated try to take back your neighborhoods, with a warning and a deadline. It comes set to normal and can be turned off in Options, The street.
  • The street has more life: people who shout, talk and act, patrols on their rounds, ambulances, and being able to surrender to the police.
  • Shops you walk through with a basket, a pantry and a fridge at home, cooking with recipes and home delivery food.
  • Robberies at any business, deals with the gangs, three jobs to choose from on the board, six new weapons and, as optional modes, working as a police officer, the police helicopter and the neighborhood war on the street.
  • Planned heists: before a robbery you hand out roles among your gang, driver, lookout, escort, safecracker and shooter, and the heist is played in phases, with your crew talking to you over the radio.
  • Real flying: helicopters and light planes have altitude and fuel, and you go from one town to another on a real crossing, with wind, a control tower, storms and an autopilot. There are hangars, you can hijack an aircraft with the radar on your tail, and with a license you can work as a commercial pilot. It comes turned on and can be turned off in Options.
  • On a server: if it restarts or the connection drops, the game waits and rejoins by itself, just as you were. Also, text roleplay channels, money limits explained with figures and purchases that are not lost.
  • Whoever runs a server can update the game with the server open: it warns, saves, updates and starts again by itself.
  • On a server, people with different versions of the game can play together, on equal terms: damage and money are decided by the server, what your version does not have reaches you as the closest thing, and your character loses nothing by changing version.
  • You can be a good person, a criminal or both, with the consequences: standing and street reputation change how you are treated, there is a criminal record, permanent contracts with a boss and promotions, studies with qualifications, businesses that grow, loans, mortgages and mild taxes, and a new story with voices in all its scenes, The Right Path, which can be completed entirely without committing a crime. It comes set to mild and can be adjusted or turned off in Options.
  • Everything new can be done with a controller, with an actions menu and a way to type without a keyboard.
  • Thirty-six new sounds, 279 in total, and all of them in Learn the sounds, by groups.
  • Updates that say why they fail, and how to update manually.
  • The details of everything, and how each new option comes, are in the full change log: in the main menu, About, What's new in this version.

Fixed

  • With the GPS autopilot, the car no longer takes hits at junctions: traffic turning right no longer pulls out on top of you, and oncoming traffic waits for you to pass before turning left.
  • Gym fights are no longer a crime: knocking down your opponent in boxing or in the martial arts night does not call the police or lower your standing.
  • Inside businesses, F1 explains the business you are in, and not your house.
  • When you press Enter at the counter, the bar or the window, the game says that you walk up and they serve you, and you go back to the business menu.
  • The police station board can be read: it says whether you are wanted, what the fine would cost you, the wanted gangs and the day's incidents. The lottery prize board and the job center's offers board can also be read.
  • Neighborhoods say who they belong to at each moment: a gang, you, at war, or the gang that took them from you. And when you conquer a neighborhood you fight the gang that rules it.
  • Places named after the neighborhood's gang, such as the hideout, change their name and description when the neighborhood becomes yours or another gang's.
  • Leaving for another town by road is no longer confusing: when you reach the exit with your vehicle, the game says that you are already there and to press Enter without getting out. Before, the GPS could send you a few meters further on, where the car can't reach. On foot, the game explains that you leave by road in a vehicle and guides you to the bus station.
  • The exit from Puerto Bravo to Valdeolmos could not be taken, because it was at the same spot as a lookout point. Two places can no longer end up on top of each other.
  • At the wheel, the GPS always takes you by road to another town, even if you have to cross another one on the way. Before, if it was not the neighboring town, it sent you to the bus.
  • The transport job with the truck is fully explained at the start: the load is already inside, it is taken by road and it is delivered by itself when you stop in front. It no longer sends you to the bus station, and G marks the destination again if you remove it.
  • The GPS detour notice always reminds you that it is canceled with Shift G.
  • The tutorials check what you do. The sailing one asks you to cast off, steer and dock, with a free practice motorboat. The jobs one asks you to do a real taxi fare. And in the first one, the radar exercise asks you to use the radar.
  • Playing with friends is no longer a mission completed by going to a bar: it is an explanation that is read on the phone.
  • No vehicle is lost when you retry a mission. In 1.7.0, if you sent your vehicle to the garage after the mission had started, failed and retried, it disappeared from the street and from the garage; and the one you took out of the garage was duplicated. Now vehicles, garages and moored boats don't go back in time when you retry, neither from the start nor from a checkpoint.
  • The O key now always gives the direction, not just the meters, and says the name of the place as it appears in the list of places.
  • On the phone, the mission you have in progress guides you to its current objective, not to the place where it started.
  • A GPS detour to a parked vehicle no longer gets stuck: it ends when you get in, and the GPS goes back to what you had before, saying so. Before, G kept pointing at the vehicle you were already in.
  • After updating, the game says where the change log really is: in the main menu, About, What's new in this version.
  • The voice generator for your own character is tested separately before it is used: if it fails on your computer, the game no longer closes, and the lines with your name are spoken by the screen reader.
  • If you had changed keyboard keys, the controller buttons are no longer mixed up.
  • In Options, Sound and music, Adjust the volume of each sound, none are missing any more.

The street, people and the police

  • Self-defense: if a mugger, a gang member or anyone comes after you, shooting them or knocking them down is no longer a crime, and it counts from the moment they rush at you, not from their first blow. Neither is stopping the people in a street incident. If there is a patrol nearby or someone calls it in, the officers go after whoever attacked you, not after you.
  • People can be heard: each person shouts and complains with their own voice, male or female, when they get scared or take a hit. And the game says who runs away or steps aside, and in which direction, without overloading you.
  • People who talk as you pass: some people walk in pairs, and when you pass by them you hear a stray line. You choose how often.
  • People on the street now have a surname, and there are more names, trades and lines.
  • People also act: someone who comes to trust you may keep you company for a while, and someone who gets fed up with you may punch you or report you.
  • Two new options when talking to someone: asking them for a favor, and suggesting they join your gang. Not everyone accepts: trust, your reputation and their personality all count.
  • With artificial intelligence mode, a new option lets the character suggest keeping you company, leaving, getting angry or joining your gang. The game always decides with its own rules, and no character gives you money or items because of what you type.
  • Patrols on their rounds: a patrol car drives around the streets even when nobody is after you. They see the crimes you commit nearby and, with traffic rules turned on, they hand out the fines.
  • Ambulances: a while after a shootout or a hit-and-run, one arrives with its siren, treats the injured and leaves.
  • Surrendering: with the police after you and an officer nearby, Shift H raises your hands. They stop shooting and arrest you. The more you are wanted, the bigger the fine and the more hours in the cells; at the highest level you lose all your weapons.
  • The police come better armed the more you are wanted: at level 4, submachine guns and shotguns; at level 5 the Borrasca Unit also comes out, an assault unit that arrives in a van with rifles.
  • Police helicopter, optional: at the highest wanted level you hear it arrive and circle overhead, and it talks to you over the loudspeaker. While it has you located you can't shake off the police. It loses you if you stick close to a building or go inside one, and it doesn't recognize you if you change vehicle without it seeing you. It can be shot down, but that costs you dearly.
  • Some enemies arrive in a vehicle: part of the escort in some contracts and the reinforcements in the last assault when you conquer a neighborhood. You hear them coming, you can shoot the vehicle before they get out, and when they get out the vehicle is left for you.
  • Many enemies shout a line when they see you, with their name first: that way you know who has seen you.
  • Fallen enemies drop ammunition for the weapon they had, if you carry the same one. It is picked up by itself when you walk over it. And as a difficulty option, they can sometimes drop the whole weapon.
  • New section in Options, The street: people, police and gangs, with all of the above. Each thing can be turned on or off.

Weapons and combat

  • Six new weapons, each with its own sound: fire axe, burst-fire pistol, pump-action shotgun, lever-action rifle, semi-automatic rifle and grenade launcher.
  • Not all weapons are bought at the gun shop: the axe is in the shops, and the pump-action shotgun and the lever-action rifle are sold second-hand at the pawn shops. Ammunition, at any gun shop.
  • Each weapon states its damage and range at the gun shop and in your weapons list, and with F1 it gives the full sheet: damage, range, rate of fire and accuracy, in words and in figures.
  • Shooting from a vehicle is explained when it matters: the first time you are shot at while at the wheel, the game reminds you that Q locks on and Control fires, without getting out. The F1 help at the wheel explains it in full.
  • Missions adjust to your escort: if you go with a lot of people, a few more and tougher enemies wait for you, up to a limit.
