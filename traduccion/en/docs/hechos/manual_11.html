People, in their place

The people in your social life aren't only on the phone. Each one is at the place where they usually are, and with opening hours turned on, only at their hours: when you go in on foot, the first option in the menu is to walk over and talk, and if you walk around inside the place, you find them there. It's the same menu as on the phone, but in person, so there you can do everything that is done face to face.

From there you can suggest they come out with you for a walk around. They really come along: they follow you on foot, you can hear their footsteps, and they get into whatever vehicle you're driving, be it a car, a motorbike or a bus. Walking around counts, and taking them to eat, to a bar, to the movies, to the fair or to the casino pleases them and brings you closer. Getting into trouble with the police doesn't. To say goodbye, take them back to where they were or tell them from their profile. Once a day, and it lasts at most three game hours.

If your partner finds out

  • If you have a partner and you meet up, spend the night, or kiss or dance with someone else, they may find out.
  • If they find out, the relationship drops a lot, and the wound lasts ten days: meanwhile, what you do together brings you half as close, and the profile reminds you how many days are left.
  • Asking them to forgive you, in person and once a day, can shorten it: if they listen, it takes off four days. If not, better not to insist that day.
  • If it happens again while the wound is still open, that's it: they leave you.

Couple plans

With your partner there are more things to do. In their profile, Make a plan together: an aimless walk around the neighborhood, going to the movies, dinner out, a boat trip at sunset, an afternoon at the beach, going shopping, a night at the fair or going dancing. Some require the town to have the right place, and the game tells you.

  • Each plan is told with a spoken scene, and it's not the same every time: none repeats until you've heard them all.
  • It brings you closer and raises your mood, more so for the ones that cost more. One a day.
  • They add to the dates, gifts and gestures that were already there.

Living together, optional

It comes turned off. It's turned on in Options, Adult content, Life as a couple. With the option turned on, your partner's profile shows Ask them to move in with you.

  • Each person decides. The relationship has to be going very well and you need to have had a good few dates; and there are people who won't leave their home for anyone, however well you're getting on. If they say no, there's no point insisting the next day.
  • If they accept, they live in one of your homes. If you have several, you choose which one when you ask them, in this town or another.
  • Moving: in your partner's profile, the option that says where you live, or in the house menu, Move to another home of yours, lets you choose another of your homes, also in another town, and you both go.
  • Giving them money to buy something: in your partner's profile, or at home, you choose how much, from fifty to five hundred euros. It brings you closer the more you give, once a day, and the next day they tell you what they bought. It works for any partner, whether they live with you or not.
  • At home: at night they're always there; during the day, only at times, because they have their own life. When you come in they greet you, or you find a note. In the house menu there's an option with their name: talk for a while, eat together, make a plan or go to their profile.
  • Eating together: they cook for both of you or, sometimes, it's your turn. It gives health and takes away hunger and thirst in realistic life. Once a day.
  • They ask you for things now and then: a delivered dinner, a plan, that you sleep at home, a piece of furniture, a small gift or some money. You have a couple of days. If you do it, it brings you closer; if you let it go, they take note.
  • Living together counts as seeing each other: the relationship doesn't cool off from not meeting up. But if you go days without showing up at home, it suffers.
  • There's one more plan, only for home: a night on the sofa, with a movie and a blanket.
  • You can ask them to move out. It doesn't end the relationship, but it cools it a lot. If you break up, they leave on their own.
  • If you bring someone else to that home and your partner is inside, it's over.

It can't be done on an online server. The numbers and phrases are in the game data.

22. Content for people over 18

It comes turned off. It's turned on in Options, Adult content, confirming that you're of legal age. Turned off, none of this appears in the game: not the places, not the missions, not the phone options. Everything is told without explicit scenes and all the characters are adults.

  • Social life, on the phone: you meet people in bars and other places. You can chat, suggest a date, give a gift, ask them to be your partner, spend the night together or break up. Affinity goes up and down with what you do, and there's jealousy if you're in more than one relationship. What is done in person and what by phone, how to meet up and what happens if your partner finds out is in the previous chapter, Social life.
  • Nightclubs: drinks, shows and paid company.
  • Substances: each one has its effect, its sound when you take it and its comedown. Abuse creates dependence, with withdrawal symptoms, and can end in an overdose. Hospitals offer detox.
  • Dealing: suppliers sell goods at a price that changes every day and in every place. On foot, carrying goods, the E key next to a pedestrian offers them a deal; they may accept, refuse it or be a plainclothes police officer.
  • Everything has an effect: on your mood, your health, your street reputation, your standing among the neighbors and your relationships.

Robbing a supplier

Substance suppliers can be robbed, as well as bought from. The option is in their menu. You need a firearm in your hand and to have nothing else going on.

  • They have a guard, and sometimes reinforcements who arrive in a vehicle once it has already started.
  • If you take them out, you pick up the goods and the cash box: you take whatever goods there are, as much as fits in your backpack, and the money.
  • They don't forget. One of these three things happens, and the game tells you which: that supplier stops selling to you for a few days; or keeps selling to you, but at a higher price, for a while; or sends people after you, who arrive a few hours later, when you're out on the street.
  • Even if the robbery goes wrong for you, the consequence stays.
  • The same supplier can't be robbed again until a few days have passed: they have no stock.

If you have a gang, the robbery can also be planned, with the Plan the heist with your gang option in that same menu: it's explained in the section Prepared heists: everyone has a role.

It can't be done on an online server. The numbers and phrases are in the game data.

A night with someone, step by step

Spending the night with someone is no longer told in two sentences: it's played. At each moment you choose what to do from three options, and the other person responds. Each person has a pace they feel like, which changes from one day to the next and isn't said: you notice it in how they react. If you get it right, they say so. If you ask them what they feel like, they tell you. And if you go faster than they want, they ask you to wait: stopping and asking them is what brings you closest.

Depending on how well you've understood each other, the night leaves you more or less mood and brings you more or less close. It's still told sensually, without explicit scenes, and a no is always respected. If you prefer it to be told in a couple of sentences, as before, turn off A night with someone, step by step, in Options, Adult content.

The lab

In a home of yours you can set up a clandestine lab for 9,000 euros, from the house menu. To work in it you need materials, which any supplier sells in batches.

  • Making it is a listening minigame. Two tones play: a fixed one, on the left, which is the sweet spot, and yours, on the right, which drifts up or down. With Up Arrow and Down Arrow you bring yours to match the fixed one and keep it there.
  • It lasts twenty-four seconds. The longer the two tones stay matched, the better the batch turns out and the more units you get, between four and sixteen.
  • If you let your tone stay very high for a long time, it can catch fire: you get burned, you lose the lab and the police come. Sometimes, on top of that, a neighbor notices the smell and calls.
  • Escape leaves it half done and the batch is lost.
  • What you make is sold like any other goods: to a supplier or on the street.

It's a trade invented for the game, like its substances: it doesn't describe any real process.

Flirting with people on the street

With adult content turned on, when you talk to a person with the J key more options appear: flirt, suggest going to your home, if you have one, or to a hotel. Each person is attracted to whoever they're attracted to, they may have a partner or not be in the mood, and it depends on how they've taken to you: no means no, and if you insist they leave. In Options, Adult content, you say who you're attracted to and, if you want, how others see you.

  • A night together can be one of four kinds, and you choose: romantic, passionate, full of laughs and games, or relaxed. With the people in your social life there are also gestures of affection: a kiss, dancing together, a massage or whispering something in their ear. It takes some trust and each one can be repeated once a day.
  • Everything is told with sensuality and without explicit scenes: when the door closes, the game moves on to the next morning.
  • Pharmacies and hospitals have condoms. In the clubs they also sell candles, massage oil, lingerie and a toy for two: each item makes one kind of night better.
  • Without protection you can catch an infection: you notice it in your mood and your health until you treat it. Pharmacies and hospitals have a free test and treatment. The V key tells you how you are.
  • In the clubs, as well as drinks and shows, you can dance on the floor and meet someone.

23. Playing with friends

You can play with other people without depending on any outside server: your own game acts as the server. You set up the game and carry on playing normally; you give your friends an address and they connect.

What is shared

  • You hear your friends where they are: the engine of what they're driving, their footsteps and their horn.
  • The radar announces them and the GPS takes you to them, even if they're in another town.
  • You send each other messages, which are read aloud. The F10 key opens the box for typing without going through the phone.
  • You can send each other money.
  • You can give each other first aid kits.
  • You can ride in the same vehicle: stand next to your friend's and press F to get in as a passenger; you go wherever they go. F again to get out.
  • Joint activities, from Play with friends: a race to a place with a hundred-euro bet each, won by whoever arrives first, and a team delivery run, in which you all carry packages around the city together and split the pay. One person proposes it, the others accept it and whoever proposed it starts it.
  • Each person keeps their own game, their missions and their progress, and can do everything they would do playing alone: missions, jobs, shopping, homes, fishing or races. Each person's city is their own: the people and traffic you hear are those of your game.

Opening your game

  1. In the game, open the phone with M and go into Play with friends.
  2. If you want, set a password: only those who know it will get in. Without a password anyone with the address can get in.
  3. Choose Open my game to my friends. The game tells you the address for anyone on your same network.
  4. In How to invite a friend you have both addresses, the home one and the internet one, and with Enter they're copied to the clipboard so you can paste them into a message.

An address looks like this: puertobravo://83.45.12.7:47810. The last number is the port.

So that friends from outside your home can get in

First of all: when you open your game, the game tries to open your router's port by itself, and tells you whether it succeeded. If it does, you don't have to do any of the following, and when you close the game it closes it again. It depends on your router allowing it; many come with it turned on. It can be turned off on the phone, in Play with friends, Open the router port automatically. If the game says it couldn't, these are the manual steps:

  • On your router you have to open port 47810, TCP protocol, to your computer. It's usually in the section on opening ports or port forwarding.
  • The first time, the Windows firewall asks whether to allow the game access: say yes.
  • If you use AdGuard, an antivirus with network protection or another connection filter, add Puerto Bravo to its exceptions. The game retries automatically when a connection drops right at the start, and reconnects if it drops in the middle of the game, but a filter that always blocks can't be bypassed.
  • Without opening the port only people on your same network will be able to get in.

Joining a friend's game

On the phone, Play with friends, Join a friend's game. Type or paste with Control V the address they gave you, and then the password, if there is one. The game remembers the last address.

While you play

  • On the phone, Play with friends shows who is there and where. With Enter on a friend you can mark them on the GPS or send them money.
  • Whoever opened the game can kick a player from that same menu.
  • Received messages can be reviewed in Review the latest messages.
  • Three sounds alert you: three rising notes when someone joins, three falling ones when someone leaves and two high notes when a message arrives.
  • The connection isn't encrypted: don't write anything you wouldn't say out loud.

When typing text

In the game's text boxes (messages, addresses, passwords, your name) each letter is spoken as you type it. Enter accepts, Escape cancels, Backspace deletes, Up Arrow reads what you've typed and Control V pastes. Passwords aren't spoken: you hear "asterisk".
