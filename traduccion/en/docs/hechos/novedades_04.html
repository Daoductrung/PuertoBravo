New in the game

  • Where you left the vehicle. With Shift L, or on the phone with Where is my vehicle, the game tells you where the vehicle you got out of is and sets it on the GPS. If you were heading somewhere else, when you reach the vehicle the GPS goes back to that destination by itself. And if you go to another town without it, it waits for you there: the game tells you in which town and on which street you left it.
  • The next intersection. Shift N tells you which street is the next one you will cross, how many meters away it is and which street you are on, on foot or at the wheel. Just that, without the rest of the information.
  • Shift V tells you only your hunger, thirst, sleep and hygiene, without having to hear your whole status.
  • The right Shift no longer makes you run or switches between walking and running. Only the left one does; the right one is kept for key combinations.
  • Download progress. While a new version or the artificial intelligence model is downloading, any key tells you the percentage and how many megabytes it has so far.
  • Downloads in the background. During those downloads, B sends them to the background and you keep playing. The game lets you know when they finish, and the new version is installed whenever you want, from Options, Updates.
  • The radio pauses when you get out. The song stops and, when you get into any vehicle, it carries on right where it left off. This works for all stations and for My music, and each station remembers its own place. If you prefer the station to keep playing without you, like a real radio, change it in Options, Sound.
  • The radio on foot. Stores sell a music player with headphones: on foot, Shift R turns it on and changes station. When you get into a vehicle, the song carries on without cutting out.
  • Music in venues. In stores, bars, gyms, repair shops and other venues, one of the game's stations plays quietly in the background; each venue has its own.
  • Gamepad: button combinations. While you hold the View, Create or Share button, the other buttons have a second function. So now, without a keyboard, you can turn on the GPS autopilot, hear how far you have left to go, the next intersection, where you left the vehicle, cancel the GPS, change station and more. They are changed in Options, Gamepad.
  • Fuel, optional and turned off by default. In Options, Combat and difficulty. Cars, motorcycles and trucks use fuel, more the faster you go; they warn you when they go into reserve and stop when the tank is empty. You refuel at gas stations without getting out, with fuel cans or by calling roadside assistance. There is a gas station in every town with cars.
  • Thirst and hygiene, as part of realistic life. Besides eating and sleeping, you have to drink and wash. You drink in bars, restaurants, stores and your kitchen; the shower is in your home and in hotels.
  • Breakdowns, optional and turned off by default. Vehicles count kilometers, get a flat tire every now and then and need an inspection from time to time. You carry spare tires, which you change from the phone.
  • Traffic rules and the points license, optional and turned off by default. Running a red light or speeding costs you a fine and points, but only if the police see you. With no points left your license is taken away, and you get it back with a course.
  • Mixed martial arts. In gyms, fight nights of three rounds in a row, each opponent tougher than the last. They pay more than boxing.
  • Accessories. In clothing stores: glasses, chains, rings, watches, caps and earrings, cheap ones and designer ones. You wear them as well as your clothes, and they lift your mood the first time you wear them.
  • More protection. Tactical clothing and a camouflage uniform, which have reinforcements, in clothing stores; and a tactical chest rig and full tactical gear in gun shops, with armor of 150 and 200.
  • New trades. Shifts as a carpenter, welder, mechanic's assistant and lifeguard at the job center. And car salesperson at the dealerships, with a salary and commission; if you like, you can also ask for the keys for a test drive and never come back.
  • Relaxed sound. A new option in Options, Sound, for computers where the audio clicks, cuts out or stutters.
  • All the game's prices are now in the data: what the cinema, the repair shop, a taxi, a bodyguard and fines cost, the salaries, and what each job pays. That is 89 fares that a mod can change.
  • The mod editor has eleven new sections: clothing, accessories, gun shop protections, workdays, gym fights, fuel, home furniture, repair shop upgrades, realism, radio on foot and in venues, and fares and prices. All of that is in the game data, and a mod can change it or add its own. Places have the gas station mark and the price per liter, and vehicles have their tank.

Help in missions

  • Following someone on foot: they no longer start walking until you find them. Before, they set off as soon as the job began and, if you were far away, you lost them before you even got there. Now the GPS takes you to them and the game tells you when they are close.
  • Chases: while they are far away, the game tells you every so often how many meters ahead they are and which way they are going, and lets you know when they are within reach. If they already got away from you, the next time they are more nervous and go slower, and if there is a time limit you get a bit more.
  • U-turn: the GPS now tells you the key, U, with the car almost stopped. And the GPS autopilot does it by itself when the destination, or the vehicle you are chasing, is behind you, instead of repeating that you should turn around.

Fixed

  • The starting weapon of a mod character did not respect the spare ammunition the mod gave it.

Version 1.6.3

Made with what you told us while testing 1.6.2, and with one big change: new voices for all the characters, who call you by your name whoever you play as.

New

  • New voices in all the scenes. Each character in the story and in the added stories has their own voice: that is seventy-four characters and seventy-four voices, instead of the six that were shared out before. All the lines are there, including those of the new missions.
  • The voices work for any main character. Before, they only played if you played as Dani in masculine. Now, with Dani, Álex, Sam or Cris, they call you by that name and speak to you in the masculine or the feminine. And the main character has a voice of their own: one male and one female voice for each of the four.
  • Voices also for the character you create. The story lines that say your name are generated on your computer, with the characters' voices, the first time you play with that name: a waiting screen of a few seconds at the start, which you can skip, and the rest while you play. When you create the character you choose your voice, you can listen to how they will call you and, if your name does not sound right, write how it is pronounced. It needs a processor from 2013 or 2015 onwards; on an older one, the screen reader says those lines.
  • When you update, the new voices replace those of the previous version. The ones you recorded yourself, in the propias folder, are not touched.
  • The voices are made with Phonon, a voice engine by Gradium, for non-commercial use. Its license comes with the game.
  • Furnishing the house. In the menu of any home of yours there are six things to buy, one per room: a sofa and a TV, a new kitchen, a bathtub, a really good bed, plants and paintings, and a workbench for the garage. Each one improves what you do in its room or how well you rest, and the workbench repairs the stored vehicles while you sleep.
  • Talking in your own words to people you already know. In the card of each person in your social life, and of your partner, there is a new option: you write whatever you like and they answer, just like when you talk to people on the street. If you are not in the same place, by phone.
  • Detours with the radar. If you are heading somewhere and you mark with B something the radar has mentioned, money on the ground or an item, the game takes it as a detour: when you get there, the GPS goes back to the destination you had by itself. With Shift G you drop the detour.
  • Create your own character. In New game, at the end of the list of characters, there is Create your own character. You give them a first name and a surname, choose whether they speak to you in the masculine or the feminine, share out eight levels among the four skills, with five at most in each, and pick a starting advantage: Savings, Toughness, Quick Hands, a switchblade, People skills or None. The story calls you by the name you wrote.
  • Main character in masculine or feminine. When you choose Dani, Álex, Sam or Cris, the game asks how you want to be spoken to. From then on, the dialogue and messages respect it: welcome in the masculine or feminine, Encarna's nephew or niece, son or daughter, stranger, boss, in the right form. It is saved with the game. Games from earlier versions stay as they were, in masculine.
  • The characters of the example mods now say whether they are he or she, and with them the game does not ask.
  • A railway line in every town with a station. It crosses the town from one side to the other and trains run along it: they come in at one end, stop at the platform, blow their horn and leave at the other end. You can hear them coming and going from the street. You can cross the track on foot; the game says train tracks when you step on it and, if a train is coming, it blows its horn and warns you; if you do not get out of the way, it runs you over.
  • By bus, from one town to another, a train catches up with you on the line that runs alongside the road and you hear it pass from one ear to the other.
  • Flights to the villages and the islands. The villages you reach by road have an airfield, with scheduled light planes to the cities and the other villages. You reach the islands and the platform by scheduled helicopter from any airport or airfield, and from their heliports you can also fly back to the villages.
  • The branches of the story cross over. Each branch remembers what you decided in the others: whoever gives you the mission comments on it at the end. When you have closed all three, the Puerto Bravo, Villaseca and San Lucero ones, the mission Three Letters arrives at the bar, and after it one more ending, out of three possible ones, depending on what your decisions add up to.
  • New lottery. Each ticket has a five-digit number and the draw is every day at nine in the evening. The game says the winning number and you check whether you have won, on the phone or wherever tickets are sold. Prizes go by the last digits: refund, two, three and four digits, and a jackpot for all five that grows every day nobody wins it. Tickets are sold at lottery offices, where you can ask for your number, and also in stores, bars and taverns.
  • Friendly fire, optional. In Options, Combat and difficulty, there is a new option: Friendly fire. By default it is set to yes, which is the realistic choice: you can hurt your dog, your bodyguards and your gang. With no, you cannot aim at them or hurt them, not even by accident.
  • Looking after the dog. Shift Y opens its menu: pet it, feed it, send it home and call it. It gets hungry over the hours and lets you know. Dog food is sold at the animal shelter and in stores.
  • Choosing how you collect from your businesses: have it brought to you in person every midnight, as before; have it paid into the bank; or go and collect it at each business yourself, for ten percent more. You change it on the phone, in Properties, or in the menu of any of your businesses.
  • Twelve new missions, in a chapter of their own: Jobs of the Region. They are standalone jobs spread around Puerto Bravo, Monteferro, Villaseca, San Lucero, Cala Brava and Fuentefría: following someone, escorting, getting in quietly, keeping watch, deliveries against the clock, a race, a robbery to order and a trip out to sea.
  • With a gamepad connected, the game tells you the buttons instead of the keys: in what it says while you play, in the tutorials and in the scenes. "Press the left trigger to lock on", instead of "press Q". It follows your button assignment and is turned off in Options, Gamepad.
  • The GPS goes back to the mission by itself. If in the middle of a mission you go off to do something else and the GPS is left without a destination, after two seconds it sets what the mission asks for again. With G you can also get it back by hand.
  • The vehicle you get out of waits for you where you left it, even if you go far away, and it is saved with the game. Before, it disappeared when you went away.
  • Easier garage: if you leave the vehicle parked near your home, the home menu offers to put it in the garage. And from the phone, in Services, your mechanic takes the vehicle you are using to one of your garages, for 100 euros.
  • The GPS autopilot stops when you arrive and says so. In a hurry, with the siren on or in a mission or job against the clock, it goes faster and does not wait at traffic lights.
  • Getting in and out no longer sounds the same in every vehicle: a motorcycle and a bicycle have no door, a truck's door is deep and a sports car's door is light. And each person has their own voice, deeper or higher, in their complaints and their shouts.
  • In the mod editor: the railway line of each town, the airfields, and conditions to combine decisions from different stories.
  • In the mod editor, the character has a new field, They talk to them, and in any text you can write what changes in square brackets, with the masculine form, a bar and the feminine form: Bienvenid[o|a], [el sobrino|la sobrina].

Fixed

  • The main menu music took several seconds to start playing. Now it comes in straight away.
  • A peace pact between gangs could be accepted several times in a row and collected each time, because the Territories menu kept showing the offer. The same happened when paying the inspector. Not anymore.
  • People on the street could be a student at sixty-four or retired at nineteen. Now their trade matches their age.
  • AI mode with the model installed in the game itself gave a Windows error on some computers: it said VCRUNTIME140.dll was missing. The game already includes those libraries and now gives them to the model's program.
  • A mission could be marked as completed without playing it. If you were arrested, or knocked out, at the very moment the last enemy or the chased vehicle went down, the final scene stayed open under the mission failed message; when you chose Retry, that old scene pushed the newly started mission through to the end. Now, if you had already done what the mission asked, it does not fail: after the police station or the hospital, its scene continues and it is completed. And a scene from a mission that has already ended cannot move another one.
  • In the missions where you steal a vehicle, lose the police and deliver it, if you left the vehicle and changed town the mission got stuck. Now it fails with its reason and you can retry it.
  • With the GPS autopilot, the car did not actually stop at the destination, and missions where you go to a place were not marked as done until you braked yourself.
  • In The Courier and the other chases, the fleeing car was hard to catch with an ordinary vehicle. Now it goes slower on easy and normal, and it eases off if it gets far ahead of you.
  • Stutters when loading music. Radio songs longer than a minute and a half now play in segments, ambient music is read without duplicating memory, changing station quickly does not pile up reads, and reading music from the disk gives way to the game.
  • The update said "The download was cut off before it finished" and you had to start from scratch. Now it resumes by itself from where it was.
  • With members of your gang beside you, the phone said you already had two bodyguards and did not let you call any.
  • Cars caught fire too soon. The main cause was the tram: it rammed any car driving along an avenue with tracks, even one beside it, and took off half its health with each hit. Now the tram brakes and waits behind a vehicle. Also, the vehicle you are driving takes less damage on easy and normal, skidding only causes damage at high speed, the game warns you that smoke is coming out at 25 percent and, if it catches fire, you have more time to get out.
  • The theft of the Centella from Autos Platino asks you to deliver it with somewhat less condition: at 30 percent instead of 40.
  • If you hit your dog by accident, it stayed marked as an enemy forever and there was no way to fix it. Now it becomes afraid of you, and you fix it by making peace: petting, food or time.
  • Hitting a bodyguard or a member of your gang left them in a strange state. Now they warn you twice and on the third time they turn against you.
  • The members of your gang who were with you were saved in the game as bodyguards and drew two salaries. Not anymore.
  • A very long recording on the My music station froze the game and could hang the whole computer. The radio loaded the whole song into memory before starting, and that is about one hundred and thirty megabytes for every minute of audio: with a one-hour recording, almost eight gigabytes. Now songs longer than five minutes are read and played in one-minute segments, one after another and without gaps, so it does not matter how long they are: a one-hour recording takes up the same as a short song and starts playing just as quickly.
  • For the same reason, a sound effect replaced by a very long file can no longer fill up the memory: at most sixty seconds of effects are loaded, ten minutes of ambiences and three of a recorded voice. No sound in the game comes close to those limits.
  • The dialogue always addressed the main character in the masculine, even if the chosen character had a woman's name.
  • The combat tutorial did not let you lock on to the dummy. Tutorials are started from the phone, wherever you are, but the dummy appeared at the shooting club while the narrator said "you are at the shooting club": if you were far away, Q answered "no enemies nearby". Now, if you are far from where a tutorial takes place, or in another town, the tutorial itself takes you to its starting point and tells you. This applies to the four that are started from the phone: combat, jobs, sea and playing with friends.
  • In that tutorial, the dummies and targets are no longer announced as enemies or as "taken down": the game says how many practice targets there are and how far away the nearest one is, and when you knock one down it tells you that another one is left and that Q locks on to it. They also do not count in your statistics or leave money on the ground.

Improved

  • Several game messages have been written so that they work for anyone: "you have no injuries", "you keep playing on your own", "if you crouch they hear you less".
  • The names of trades, ranks and some achievements change with the main character: ambulance driver, veteran, champion, in the feminine when it applies.
  • The name you write for your character is not recorded in the session log.
