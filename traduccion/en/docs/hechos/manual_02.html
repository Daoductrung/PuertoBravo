At any time

  • C: where am I: street, neighborhood and which way I am facing. With Shift it adds the coordinates.
  • V: status: health, money, weapon, wanted level and vehicle.
  • T: time, day and weather.
  • O: objective of the current mission or job: first says where it is, how many meters away, in which direction and, if it moves, where it is heading, and then the rest. If the objective moves or the clock is running, one press says only that, and two in a row read the whole explanation. In a heist planned with your gang it also says the phase and who does what; on a commercial flight or with a hijacked aircraft, where you have to take it.
  • Shift O: back to the objective of the mission or job: drops any detour, sets it on the GPS and, if it is within range, leaves it selected on the radar. It always does the same thing.
  • N: description of the place or neighborhood you are in.
  • Shift N: next intersection: which street is the next one you are going to cross in the direction you are facing or driving, and how many meters away it is.
  • Shift V: only hunger, thirst, sleep and hygiene, with realistic life turned on.
  • Shift L: where the vehicle you got out of is: it says so and sets it on the GPS. If you were going somewhere else, when you reach the vehicle the GPS goes back to that destination on its own. If you have more vehicles, it says how many: they are on the phone, in My vehicles.
  • K: which way you are facing or driving, and plays a tone from the north to help you get your bearings.
  • F10: when playing with friends or on a server, type a message: it is read aloud to the others. To open your game to your friends or join one of theirs, open the phone with M and go into Play with friends.
  • Shift F10: on a server with roleplay channels, speak in character: only those near you hear it.
  • Control F10: on a server with roleplay channels, describe what your character is doing: you type «opens the door» and those nearby hear your name and «opens the door».
  • F9: map explorer: goes through the town with a cursor, intersection by intersection, without moving your character. Tab says the places next to the cursor and Enter sets the destination.
  • Tab and Shift Tab: radar: goes through what is around you, from nearest to farthest.
  • B: points the GPS and the beacon at the last thing the radar said. If you were already going somewhere, it is a detour: when you get into that vehicle, reach that person, reach that item or pick it up, or reach that place, the GPS goes back on its own to what you had, and says so.
  • F6: changes the radar filter: everything, places, vehicles, people or items.
  • L: list of places in the city to set a destination.
  • G: repeats the GPS direction. In a mission, with no destination set, it sets again what the mission asks for; with no mission, it brings back the last destination you cleared.
  • Shift G: clears the GPS destination. If you were on a detour, the GPS goes back to what you had; another press clears that too.
  • Control G: brings back the last destination or detour you cleared with Shift G, without having to look for it again. If you are now going somewhere else, it comes in as a detour.
  • M: opens the phone: missions, places, map explorer, markers, services, skills, achievements, properties and statistics. In Life, reputation and consequences you find how the city sees you, how the street sees you and what you gain or lose because of it, with your record, your contract, your studies, your businesses and what you are going to be charged.
  • Enter: interact: enter a place, start a mission, pick up an item or talk.
  • F: get into a vehicle or get out.
  • Shift F: on a server, pull another player out of their vehicle and keep it: they have to be going slowly and be next to you.
  • Q: locks on to the nearest enemy; press it again to move to the next one. Shift Q locks on to anyone.
  • Numbers 1 to 9: choose a weapon. Comma moves to the next one and Shift comma to the previous one.
  • I: opens the list of your weapons to choose one.
  • P: at sea, casts the rod to fish; when you hear a bite, press Space. In emergency vehicles, turns on the siren.
  • Z: uses a first aid kit.
  • Shift H: surrender: with the police after you and an officer nearby, you raise your hands. The officers stop shooting and come to arrest you; don't move or attack. The higher the wanted level, the bigger the fine and the more hours in the cells, and at the highest level you lose all your weapons. Another press lowers your hands.
  • Page Up and Page Down: review previous messages. Period repeats the last one.
  • Control Page Up and Control Page Down: go to the first message in the history and to the last. In Options, Voice, you choose whether each new message takes you to the end.
  • F1: this help.
  • F2: switches between assisted and free driving.
  • F3: switches on-foot movement between compass and turns.
  • F4: turns the GPS sound beacon on or off.
  • F5: quick save, outside missions.
  • F7 and F8: turn the music volume down and up.
  • F12: puts a mark in the session log. Press it when you notice something odd: that way, whoever reviews the log will know when it happened.
  • Escape: pause menu.

On foot

  • Arrows: walk. In compass mode each arrow goes toward a compass point: up is north, right is east. In turns mode, left and right turn 45 degrees and up moves forward.
  • Left Shift with the arrows: run. Right Shift doesn't make you run or switch between walking and running. Combinations, such as Shift N, work with either of the two and don't change your pace.
  • W, A, S and D: directional scanner: says what is in front, to the left, behind and to the right, and how far away the wall is.
  • Space: attack or shoot. With automatic weapons, hold it down.
  • R: reload.
  • Shift R: the music player with headphones, if you have bought it: turns it on and changes station, so you can listen to the radio and your music on foot. One of the stations is off.
  • X: crouch or stand up. When crouching you get hit less, but you move more slowly.
  • Shift X: take cover behind a wall or a parked vehicle, or leave cover. In cover you get hit much less; if you move, you leave it.
  • Backspace: slow time: everything goes slower while you have focus left. Another press turns it off.
  • H: shout to scare off pedestrians. On duty as a police officer, orders suspects to stop.
  • E: with a weapon in your hand, rob the pedestrian next to you.
  • Y: if you have a dog, you ask it to sniff out the nearest item.
  • Shift Y: your dog's menu: how it is, pet it, feed it, send it home or call it.
  • J: talk to the person next to you. Each one has their own name, job and personality: you can say hello, ask about a place, buy them a coffee, make friends or type whatever you want to say to them.
  • Shift J: when playing with friends or on a server, says how the nearest player is dressed: their clothes and accessories. On a server with roleplay channels, it also reads their character sheet, if they have written one. It only says it when you ask.
  • U: walk on your own: with a destination set on the GPS, your character follows the route on their own until they arrive. Hold Shift to make them run. Any arrow, or U again, stops them.

At the wheel, by boat and by helicopter

  • Up Arrow: accelerate.
  • Down Arrow: brake and, when stopped, reverse.
  • Space: handbrake.
  • Left and Right Arrows: in assisted driving they turn on the turn signal: the car will turn on its own at the next intersection. In free driving they turn the steering wheel.
  • A and D: in assisted driving, hold them to change lanes: A moves into the oncoming lane to overtake and D pulls you over to the curb. Flying a helicopter or a light plane, with real flight, D climbs and A descends.
  • Automatic braking: in assisted driving the car brakes on its own if there is a vehicle ahead in your lane. It can be turned off in Options.
  • U: U-turn, with the car almost stopped. With the GPS autopilot, it makes it on its own when the destination is behind you.
  • Y: GPS autopilot: the car drives itself to the destination set; it accelerates, brakes, turns, waits at traffic lights and stops when it arrives.
  • E: in free driving, straightens the car toward the nearest compass point.
  • W: current speed.
  • H: horn.
  • R: changes radio station. Shift R turns off the radio.
  • Control: shoot from the vehicle without getting out, with a firearm in your hand: first Q locks on to someone near you, and Control shoots them. I changes weapon. With an automatic weapon, hold it down.
  • J: starts or ends a job with the vehicle: taxi, ambulance, bus, patrol car or truck. If you are a police officer, in a patrol car it starts or ends your shift.
  • Helicopter: and light plane: they fly over buildings and traffic and are flown by hand, like a boat. With real flight, Up Arrow takes off, D climbs, A descends and a note tells you the altitude; to land, slow down and press F. When you reach the edge of the town, or with Enter at a heliport or an airport, you choose a destination and make the crossing: there, the arrows control heading and altitude, Tab is the autopilot, Enter tells you how the flight is going, N speeds it up and F1 explains it. With real flight turned off in Options, you always fly at the same altitude and Enter at a heliport takes you there in one jump.
  • At sea: boats are always steered by hand: left and right turn the helm and the heading is announced. Enter next to a pier lets you dock.

Walking around inside a house, a venue or a store

  • Left Arrow and Right Arrow: go to the next room, hall or aisle. You hear your footsteps toward that side and the game says where you arrive.
  • Enter: use the spot where you are. In the kitchen at home, put away the shopping, open the fridge or the pantry and cook. In an aisle of a food store, see its products: Enter or Right Arrow puts one in the basket and Left Arrow puts it back. At the checkout, pay for the basket.
  • C: in a food store, what you have in the basket and how much it adds up to. With a controller, X or Square.
  • F1: where you are, what Enter does there and where you have to go to be served.
  • Escape: leave. In a food store, with something in the basket, it first asks what you want to do with it: pay, leave it, take it without paying or keep shopping.

6. Playing with a controller

The game recognizes Xbox controllers, PlayStation controllers, DualShock 4 and DualSense, and compatible ones on its own. Connect it by cable or by Bluetooth, before or after opening the game: it is announced when detected. Controller and keyboard can be used at the same time. Everything the keyboard does can be done with the controller.

The game's help, with F1 or with the Y or Triangle button inside a menu, says what each button does using your controller's names.

In the menus

  • D-pad or left stick: move. A or Cross: choose. B or Circle: go back.
  • Y or Triangle: help for the option. X or Square: repeat. LB and RB, or L1 and R1: review previous messages.
  • D-pad left and right: change the value of an option. In the aisle of a food store, right puts the product in the basket and left puts it back.

Walking around inside a house, a venue or a store

  • D-pad or left stick to the sides: go to the next room, hall or aisle. A or Cross: use the spot where you are. B or Circle: leave.
  • X or Square: what you have in the basket, in a food store. On the keyboard it is C.
  • View button, or Create or Share: where you are and what you can do there.
  • While you cook, A or Cross skips the wait.

On foot

  • Left stick: walk in the direction you push it; all the way, run.
  • Right stick: directional scanner. Push it to one side and it tells you what is there.
  • A or Cross: interact. Y or Triangle: get into a vehicle. X or Square: reload. B or Circle: crouch.
  • Right trigger or R2: attack. Left trigger or L2: lock on to the enemy. LB and RB, or L1 and R1: change weapon.
  • D-pad up: where am I. D-pad down: status. D-pad to the sides: radar.
  • Press the left stick: repeat the GPS. Press the right one: first aid kit.
  • View or Share button: phone. Menu or Options button: pause. Touchpad: map explorer.

Combinations: a second function on each button

It works the same with an Xbox controller as with a PlayStation one. The View button on Xbox, which is the small one on the left, next to the Xbox button; or Create or Share on PlayStation, does two things. Pressed and released, it opens the phone, as always. Held, it turns the other buttons into a second function: hold View and press another button.

On foot, holding View:

  • A or Cross: walk on your own following the GPS. B or Circle: talk to the person next to you.
  • X or Square: GPS to the last thing the radar said. Y or Triangle: where your vehicle is, and set it on the GPS.
  • D-pad up: the next intersection. D-pad down: hunger, thirst, sleep and hygiene.
  • D-pad left: list of places. D-pad right: the music player with headphones, turn on and next station.
  • LB or L1: which way you are facing. RB or R1: the time. Press the left stick: cancel the GPS. Press the right one: list of weapons.
  • Left trigger or L2: surrender to the police, and lower your hands. Right trigger or R2: the actions menu.
  • Share button, on the Xbox controllers that have it, or touchpad or microphone button, on PlayStation: how the player next to you is dressed and, on a server with roleplay channels, their character sheet. If your controller doesn't have that button, it is also in the actions menu.

At the wheel, holding View:

  • A or Cross: the GPS autopilot, on and off. B or Circle: the speed.
  • X or Square: repeat the GPS direction, with the meters left to arrive. Y or Triangle: the mission objective, with the distance.
  • D-pad up: the next intersection. D-pad down: hunger, thirst, sleep and hygiene.
  • D-pad left: turn off the radio. D-pad right: next station.
  • LB or L1: GPS to the last thing the radar said. RB or R1: which way you are heading. Press the left stick: cancel the GPS. Press the right one: the time.
  • Left trigger or L2: surrender to the police, with the vehicle stopped. Right trigger or R2: the actions menu.
  • Share button, on the Xbox controllers that have it, or touchpad or microphone button, on PlayStation: list of places. It is also in the actions menu.

While you hold View, the other buttons don't do their normal function: you don't brake or shoot by accident. All the combinations can be changed in Options, Gamepad: Reassign the on-foot and driving combinations.

The actions menu

The controller has fewer buttons than the keyboard has keys. Whatever doesn't fit on a button or a combination is in the actions menu: hold View, or Create or Share, and press the right trigger, or R2. It works on foot and at the wheel. It is a menu like the others: you move with the D-pad, choose with A or Cross, go back with B or Circle, and Y or Triangle explains the option. Only the actions that apply at that moment appear, and the ones that best fit the situation come first.

  • On duty as a police officer: order suspects to stop. Someone who surrenders is handcuffed with A or Cross, next to them.
  • Shout to scare off pedestrians, rob the pedestrian next to you, take cover and slow time.
  • On a server: speak in character, describe what your character is doing, type a message, check how the player next to you is dressed and read their character sheet, and pull another player out of their vehicle. When playing with friends, type a message.
  • Lock on to anyone, the list of weapons and the first aid kit, which have no button at the wheel, and your substances with adult content.
  • If you have a dog: have it sniff around, and its menu.
  • At the wheel, in free driving: straighten the vehicle. At sea or in an emergency vehicle: fish or the siren.
  • Objective, back to the mission objective on the GPS and the radar, bring back the last destination you cleared from the GPS, list of places, map explorer, description of the place, where your vehicle is, where you are with coordinates and the radar filter.
  • Repeat the last message. To review the previous ones, inside this menu or any other, LB and RB, or L1 and R1.
  • Switch between assisted and free driving, and between compass and turns; the GPS beacon; quick save; turn the music down and up, without the menu closing; and the mark in the session log.

That way, everything you do with the keyboard can be done with the controller. If you change the combinations and leave the actions menu without a button, you can put it back in Options, Gamepad.

Typing with the controller

When the game asks for text, such as a message for other players, a vehicle's name or your character sheet, with the controller you type with a letter wheel:

  • D-pad or left stick, up and down: go through the letters, and the game says each one. Holding the stick moves through them quickly.
  • D-pad right: types the chosen letter. D-pad left: deletes the last one.
  • LB or L1: a space. RB or R1: switches between lower case, upper case, numbers and symbols.
  • Y or Triangle: reads what you have typed. View button, or Create or Share: says all these buttons.
  • A or Cross: accept. B or Circle: cancel.

So you don't have to type, in some places there are suggestions: X or Square moves from one to the next and types it in, and with A or Cross you accept it as it is or after editing it. On the keyboard you do it with Tab. There are ready-made phrases when typing a message to the other players, speaking in character, describing an action and speaking out of character; and random names, different each time, when naming a vehicle. The phrases and names can be changed with the mod editor.

At the wheel, by boat and by helicopter

  • Right trigger or R2: accelerate. Left trigger or L2: brake and reverse. B or Circle: handbrake.
  • Left stick to the sides: a tap turns on the turn signal in assisted driving; in free driving, in boats and in helicopters it is the steering wheel.
  • Right stick to the sides: change lanes. Flying a helicopter or a light plane, right climbs and left descends. Up: next station. Down: fish at sea or siren.
  • On a flight crossing: D-pad up and down, altitude; D-pad left and right, heading; X or Square, autopilot; A or Cross, how the flight is going; Y or Triangle, speed up; LB or L1, repeat the tower; RB or R1, or B or Circle, the flight menu; and the View, Create or Share button, the help.
  • X or Square: horn. Y or Triangle: get out. A or Cross: interact and, with the line cast, reel in.
  • LB or L1: lock on to a target. RB or R1: shoot. Press the left stick: U-turn. Press the right one: job.

Vibration

The controller vibrates when you are hurt, in crashes and explosions, when your wanted level goes up, when you reach a destination and, above all, when a fish bites. In free driving, the curb vibrates on the matching side. Vibration reinforces the sound, it never replaces it, and it can be turned off.

The buttons, as the game says them

With a controller connected, what the game tells you while you play, and what the tutorials and scenes explain, names the button that does each thing instead of the key: «press the left trigger to lock on to it», instead of «press Q». It follows your mapping: if you change a button, the game says the new one. What is on a combination is said with it: «with the View button held plus the left trigger», or «with the Create or Share button held plus the L2 button» on a PlayStation controller. What has no button or combination is still said with its key, because the keyboard works the same with the controller connected: that is in the actions menu. When walking around a store, when cooking, on a server's waiting screen and when typing, the game also says your controller's buttons. The lines that change are read by your screen reader, not the recorded voice. You turn it off in Options, Gamepad, with Say the gamepad buttons instead of the keys. The menus and this help still name the keys; F1, or the Y button in a menu, says what each button does.

Changing the buttons

In Options, Gamepad, you can see what each button does, reassign any of them on foot and at the wheel, test the vibration and go back to the original mapping. The mapping is saved with your options.

7. Driving

Assisted driving, the default option

The car follows its lane on its own. You decide the speed and where to turn. Before each intersection the game tells you which street you are crossing; if you want to turn, press left or right and you will hear the turn signal. If you press nothing, you go straight on.

  • Proximity sensor: a beep that speeds up when there is a vehicle in front of you.
  • Automatic braking: the car brakes on its own so as not to ram the one in front. Hold A to overtake using the oncoming lane. It can be turned off.
  • GPS autopilot, with Y: the car drives itself. Set a destination on the GPS, get into the car and press Y: it accelerates to city speed, brakes if there is a vehicle in front, waits at red lights, turns at intersections following the route and stops when it arrives, and says so: that way you can go in, deliver or carry on with the mission without braking yourself. It stops at the traffic light line whatever the vehicle, including a bus or a truck, which brake worse: it starts braking earlier the heavier and faster it is, and if the light is going to turn red before it has time to cross, it stays put. If you are in a hurry, the autopilot goes faster: with the siren on or in a mission against the clock, it also doesn't wait at traffic lights; in a job against the clock, such as the bus, the taxi or a delivery, it does wait, because running a red light with the police in front would cost you a fine and license points. You don't have to touch anything. With any arrow or with Space you take control while you hold it, and with Y again you turn it off. It works in assisted driving and on land, with any land vehicle, including the bicycle. If you leave it on in Options, when you get into a vehicle the game reminds you that it is on and why it doesn't start: because you are in free driving or because no destination is set. For chases and races you still need to drive yourself.

Free driving

You hold the steering wheel. A low rumble warns you, on the matching side, when you go off the roadway. E straightens the car toward the nearest compass point. You can go off-road through parks and beaches.

Where you left the vehicle

The vehicle you get out of waits for you where you left it, however far you go. If later you don't remember where it was, press Shift L, or open the phone and choose Where is my vehicle: the game says how many meters away it is and on which street, and sets it on the GPS. If you go to another town without it, by train, by bus or by plane, it stays where it was: from outside, the game tells you in which town and on which street you left it, and when you come back it is still there.

This applies to the last vehicle you used, whoever it belongs to. If it is just any vehicle, you lose it when you take another one. The ones that are yours are never lost: the next section explains this.
