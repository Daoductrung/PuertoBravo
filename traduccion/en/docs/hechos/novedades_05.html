New

  • Playable tutorial. The mission Learn to get around, which starts at Grandma's Apartment, teaches you in three short exercises how to find your bearings, how to use the GPS and the radar, and how to drive. You cannot fail it.
  • Four more tutorials, on the phone, in Missions, under Learn to play: combat with dummies that do not fight back, jobs with a test taxi route, the sea, and playing with friends. While they last, the police do not respond.
  • Checkpoints in missions. If you fail a long mission, you can retry from the last stage you had reached, instead of starting it all over again.
  • Stereo ambiences. Ambient sounds and rain are played just as they were recorded, without converting them to mono. Anyone who makes a pack with surround ambiences will hear them in surround. The sounds guide explains how to prepare them.
  • Surround sound engine, with binaural sound. With headphones you can tell whether something sounds in front of you or behind you, not just to the left or right. It also mixes the sound outside the program, so it does not stutter when lots of things play at once. It is optional: you choose it in Options, Sound and music, Sound engine, and it applies when you reopen the game. The classic engine is still the default, and it is always used on computers with an old processor and with mono sound.
  • With the surround engine, each place has its own echo: almost none in the countryside and at sea, a little in the street, more among warehouses, long in the mountains and short inside a house or a venue. Things that fly sound above you, and if you are flying, the city sounds below you. It can be turned off in Options, Sound and music.
  • New sound pack, Envolvente (surround). It has the sounds of the realistic pack and eleven ambiences truly recorded in stereo, one minute each: city, neighborhood, port, beach, sea, countryside, mountain, park, industrial area, night and rain. It has the same files as the other packs, and its orientation sounds are the default ones. You choose it in Options, Sound and music. All the recordings are in the public domain, and where each one comes from is written in the pack's CREDITOS.txt file.
  • Taxi and bus trips are no longer instant: you hear the journey, with the engine, the road and the radio. They last between twelve seconds and a minute depending on the distance, and with Enter you arrive straight away. It can be turned off in Options, Movement and driving.
  • Mono sound, in Options, Sound and music: the same in both ears, for people who hear with only one.
  • The ambience and the music go down for a moment every time the voice speaks, so it is easier to understand. It can be turned off in Options, Sound and music.
  • The first time you enter a neighborhood, the game describes it. It can be turned off in Options.
  • An option for menus to say the position of each option, for example three of nine.
  • Adjustable gamepad vibration strength, from twenty to one hundred percent.
  • Remappable keyboard keys. In Options, Keyboard, you choose which key does each thing while you play. The two keys are swapped, so no function is left without a key, and the F1 help tells you the keys you have set. If you want, your keys also work in menus and dialogs, and the left Shift can be moved to another key.
  • Six new mission types, designed to be played by ear: following someone by the sound of their footsteps without being noticed, escorting someone to a place, reaching a guarded point without being heard, passing through several places in order against the clock, watching a place without being seen, and racing against rivals whose engines you can hear.
  • Seventeen new missions that use them: six in the main story, in the chapter Jobs by Ear, which start at the Puerto Bravo newspaper; six in Bahía del Ocaso, which start at El Faro del Ocaso; and one in each of the mods Costa Dorada, Valdeolmos, Peñas Altas, Isla de los Vientos and Neon Nights.
  • Vehicle upgrades. At any repair shop you can upgrade the vehicle you bring: engine, brakes and armor, with three levels each. The upgraded engine goes faster and sounds higher. The upgrades stay with that vehicle and are kept in the garage.
  • Your own gang. With at least one territory you can give your gang a name and recruit people, from the phone, in Territories. They follow you, get into your vehicle and fight by your side. Each territory lets you keep one more, up to six, and they are paid a salary every midnight.
  • You can walk around inside your homes: living room, kitchen, bathroom, bedroom and garage, going from one room to another with the arrow keys. In the kitchen there is something in the fridge once a day.
  • You can also walk around inside venues: bars, stores, banks, hospitals, repair shops, casinos and almost all the others. In the venue's menu, the option Walk around inside the place lets you go from room to room, sit down, listen in on what people are saying or go back to the counter.
  • Voices in all the stories. The scenes of the main story and of all the mods that come with the game now have voices, computer-generated, when you play as Dani. They are turned off in Options, Voice.
  • Decisions with consequences and three endings. At the end of the chapter Jobs by Ear, the mission The councilman's envelope asks you to decide what you do with the evidence, and depending on what you choose, the missions that follow, your money, your fame and how you get on with Los Calaveras all change. Each option leads to a different ending.
  • Relationship with the gangs. A gang that respects you does not attack you in the street or try to take neighborhoods from you; one that is out to get you does. You can see it on the phone, in Territories.
  • Conquered neighborhoods come to life: they give you fame when you conquer them, other gangs attack them and you have to defend them or let your gang do it, some gangs offer pacts and the police put you on their list.
  • Journal on the phone: how far you are in the story, the decisions you have made and the missions you have done. From there you can replay any mission. If you have not played for days, when you load the game it reminds you where you were.
  • Skipping a mission: if you fail it three times, you can skip it. It counts as done, with no reward.
  • Slow time, with the Backspace key: everything goes slower while you have concentration left.
  • Taking cover, with Shift X, behind a wall or a parked vehicle: you take much less damage.
  • Weapon upgrades at gun shops: silencer and extended magazine.
  • Fine or bribe: with a low wanted level and enough money, when an officer catches you, you can pay the fine, try to bribe them or let yourself be arrested.
  • Hit confirmation, with a high-pitched tap and a gamepad vibration.
  • Seven and a half, with a Spanish deck of cards, in bars and taverns.
  • New game keeping what you earned, in the main menu, and three difficulty modifiers in the options.
  • When you open your game to your friends, the game tries to open the port on your router by itself, and tells you whether it managed to. When you close it, it closes the port.
  • Separate volume for the help signals: beacon, radar, aiming tone, proximity sensor, traffic lights and place sounds.
  • When you turn on a signal in the options, you hear a sample of how it sounds.
  • Running with one key press, without holding the key, for people who cannot or do not want to hold it.
  • How much the game explains when it speaks: little, normal or a lot. And speed, pitch and volume of the Windows voice.
  • Helicopter flights with a pilot also have a journey.
  • With adult content, the people in your social life are in their places and not only on the phone: you can talk to them there and ask them to go out with you for a stroll, on foot or in your vehicle.
  • With adult content, a night with someone is played step by step: you decide what you do and the other person responds. Listening to what they want is what makes it go well. No explicit scenes, as always, and it can be left as before in the options.
  • With adult content, a clandestine lab in one of your homes: making the goods is a minigame played by ear, keeping a tone in its place. It brings money and trouble.
  • Tram in the four cities: a line that goes round the city with stops in every neighborhood, sixteen in Puerto Bravo. You get on at a stop, the tram announces each one, and you get off wherever you want with Enter.
  • Tram tracks in the streets, and trams running along them: you hear them coming, passing and stopping at each stop. You can cross the tracks on foot; when you step on them the game says so and your footsteps sound like metal. If a tram is coming and you are on the tracks, it rings its bell; if you do not get out of the way, it runs you over.
  • Near the station you hear the train coming in, and near the airport, the plane taking off.
  • Train: every town you reach overland has its own train station, separate from the bus station, and from each one you can go to all the others, including those of the mods.
  • An airport on the outskirts of the four cities: a passenger plane for long journeys, and rental light planes and helicopters that you fly yourself.
  • Four decisions open different branches of the story, each with its own missions and its own ending, and some leave consequences that last forever, such as an income every midnight.
  • Swimming and diving in the sea. With F you jump off the boat, you swim with the arrow keys, and X takes you underwater: underwater you can hold out for about thirty seconds and sometimes you find something on the bottom.
  • Light plane: faster and cheaper than the helicopter.
  • Dominoes, the coin guessing game and slot machines in bars and taverns, as well as seven and a half.
  • More decisions: eight in the main story and one in six of the mods.
  • Fourteen new sounds, in all the packs: train, train whistle, tram, tram bell, light plane, passenger plane, takeoff, swim stroke, underwater, the two footsteps on the tracks, and the signatures of the tram stop, the train station and the airport. In the recording packs they are real public domain recordings. The sounds guide lists them.
  • In Services, on the phone, you can dismiss your bodyguards.
  • The mod editor lets you create missions with those six step types, decisions with branches and endings, mark a mission as a tutorial and add enemies that do not attack.

Fixed

  • The game closed with an error when you arrived late with a package in the courier job. Fixed, and all the other trades have been checked so the same thing cannot happen.
  • The police were too overwhelming on foot. Now the first patrol takes a few seconds to come out and comes from farther away, there is a cap on patrols according to the wanted level, one at levels one and two and up to four at level five, and on foot you only need to get half a block away for them to lose sight of you.
  • With lots of patrols, the sirens overloaded the sound and the game stuttered. Now the sirens share out the volume, patrols no longer pile up without limit and the game does not spend processing on sounds that are too far away to be heard.
  • Walking and running are a bit faster.
  • The Options, Gamepad menu did not have the Go back option. Now every menu in the game that you leave with Escape also has Go back at the end.

Improved

  • Sounds that are not recorded at 44,100 hertz are now converted without losing quality in the high frequencies.
  • The manual explains that the voices in the scenes are computer-generated.
  • The voices that come with the game are now in data\voces, in one subfolder per story. Your recordings go in data\voces\propias, which updates do not touch. The voces_serie folder from earlier versions is no longer used and the game deletes it by itself.
  • Sound effects change from WAV to FLAC, in all the packs. FLAC compresses without loss, so they sound exactly the same, and they take up 55 megabytes instead of 141: the game download drops by about 85 megabytes. When you update, the game deletes the old WAV files that are no longer needed and keeps the sounds you changed yourself. Music, radio and voices stay in MP3 and OGG, because they were already compressed and changing their format would lower their quality. If you put a recording of your own with the name of a game sound, yours now always plays, without having to delete the original.
  • With a pack of long ambiences, like Envolvente, each ambience is loaded when you enter its neighborhood instead of loading them all at startup. The game uses about two hundred megabytes less memory.
  • In Learn the sounds, when you press Enter only the chosen sound plays, without the menu click on top of it.
  • Auto walk, with U, no longer gets stuck against a wall when turning a corner, and the GPS does not consider the route finished until you step onto the sidewalk, in front of the door.
  • If you are crouching without realizing it, the game tells you when you try to run, and so does the V key.
  • Collectibles give a prize and some fame for every five.
  • Saved games carry a format number: those from earlier versions are converted by themselves when you load them.
