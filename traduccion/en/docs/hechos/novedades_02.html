Territories and reconquest of neighborhoods

  • Conquering is no longer the end: playing offline, when you hold all the neighborhoods of a city, the gangs you defeated regroup and, after a few days of calm, try to take them back. With four neighborhoods or more they already start to move, more slowly.
  • It also applies to games that already had everything conquered: when you load them there are a few days of calm and a notice, only once, explaining how to defend yourself.
  • When a gang attacks a neighborhood you are always warned and you have a few hours: go there and defend it in person, in waves and with your gang at your side, or send your crew from the phone. If you do nothing, you lose it, with its daily money and some reputation, and you will have to reconquer it.
  • When you send your crew, the game tells you the odds beforehand and the result with its numbers afterwards.
  • New menu on the phone, in Territories: Neighborhood defense. It states the threat, the loyalty and the defense of each neighborhood, and everything is done from there.
  • A standing guard in each neighborhood, who is paid every day and defends it alone if you don't arrive; reinforcements paid once; and informants, who warn you of the attack in advance, with the gang, the neighborhood and the time.
  • Truces: pay a gang and it leaves you alone for a few days. They can be broken, by them or by you.
  • Preemptive strike: raid the hideout of a gang that is regrouping, in a short mission that changes every time, and crush its strength.
  • Loyalty of each neighborhood: it drops if days go by without you setting foot there and rises when you visit it, defend it or invest in it. The game warns you before it drops too far. A loyal neighborhood defends itself better and tells you what is being planned.
  • Sometimes two gangs team up for a big strike, and it is announced. A gang that is driven off several times is dismantled for a long time, but someone always ends up coming: an outside gang may settle in the region.
  • Rules to keep it fair: there are never two attacks at once, there are always days of rest between one and the next, the deadline does not run while you are on a mission, and nobody loses a neighborhood by sleeping for a night.
  • While an attack is going on, the objective key says which neighborhood is under attack and how much time you have left.
  • Negotiating with the gangs, on the phone, in Territories: give a neighborhood back to them in exchange for money, buy one from them without firing a shot, or charge them a daily tribute for letting them stay. What they answer depends on your strength and on how you get along, and you can haggle.
  • Picking a fight with a gang without conquering anything, also in Territories: they come after you wherever you are, with no neighborhoods involved.
  • In Territories, the first option explains how territories work: what they are for, how they are conquered, how they are defended and what happens if you lose them.
  • New optional mode: neighborhood war on the street. Each neighborhood belongs to a gang and its members walk its streets. A neighborhood is conquered by going there and beating three waves, with your gang at your side, and the neighborhoods that are attacked are defended in person. With the mode turned off, everything stays on the phone, as always.

Vehicles

  • Your vehicles are no longer lost. The ones you buy, the ones you upgrade at a repair shop, the ones you take out of a garage and the ones you name are yours: they stay where you park them even if you move away or take another one, in any town, and they are saved with the game. If you had an upgraded vehicle in an earlier game, it becomes yours.
  • New section on the phone, My vehicles: all of yours, with their name, model, condition, upgrades and where each one is. From there you can mark any of them on the GPS, ask for it to be brought to you, send it to a garage without going to get it, or rename it.
  • Shift L still takes you to the last vehicle you used, and now it tells you how many more you have.
  • Six of your vehicles fit on the street. If you go over that, your mechanic takes the oldest one to a garage; if there is no room, he warns you and you don't lose any.
  • Work vehicles you upgrade, such as an ambulance or a taxi, are also yours. You can't keep a mission vehicle, and the repair shop tells you so before charging.
  • If you are knocked down or arrested in another town, or you replay a mission, your vehicle stays parked where it was instead of disappearing.
  • Give your vehicles a name: in your house menu, at the repair shop or in My vehicles. The game says it when you get in, when you locate it and in the lists.
  • Missions tell you about the vehicle before you accept them, in the summary and when you reach the contact's door: whether they give you a vehicle, whether you have to take a specific one, or whether you can do it with your own. And they warn you when there is a chase for which it is best to bring a fast one.
  • When a mission gives you a vehicle, yours stays parked and safe, and the game tells you so.
  • Five transport and delivery missions can be done with your own vehicle: drive it to where the mission vehicle is and stop next to it.
  • Gang driver: if you have someone recruited, ask from the phone, in Services, or from your gang menu, for them to come and pick you up with a vehicle from your garage or one of the gang's, whichever you choose. They take a while to arrive, honk the horn, you get in as a passenger and they drive to the destination you set. You can shoot along the way, they wait for you if you get out and you dismiss them whenever you want.
  • Four new upgrades at the repair shop: reinforced tires, reinforced suspension and bumpers, exhaust silencer and bigger fuel tank.
  • At the car dealership you choose which car you take for a test drive.
  • When you retry or replay a mission, or go back to a checkpoint, the vehicle you were in, or the one you had just left next to you, comes with you and is parked beside you. The game says so, and reminds you how many you have on the street and in the garages.
  • If you were on a boat of yours when you retried, it goes back to a mooring. And if during the mission you bought a house and kept a vehicle in its garage, that vehicle goes to another garage or stays on the street in your name.

Home, shops and cooking

  • Shopping by walking around the store: in food shops, supermarkets, markets and gas stations, when you walk around inside you pick up a basket and go from aisle to aisle with the arrow keys. Each aisle has its own products. Enter puts a product in the basket, Left Arrow puts it back, C says what you have, and you pay at the checkout.
  • If you don't have enough money, the shopkeeper tells you how much is missing and you can put something back right there. If you walk out with a full basket, the game asks: pay, leave the basket or take it without paying. That last one is shoplifting: if you are seen, the police are after you and you are not allowed into that shop for a couple of days.
  • If you don't want to walk around, you have the same thing in the shop menu, in Buy takeout food for home. And everything else stays the same.
  • Each kind of shop sells its own things at its own price: the supermarket has everything, the market has fresh food and is cheaper, the corner shop has no meat or fish, and the gas station only drinks and snacks, more expensive. Every day there is a special offer, and some product that is sold out.
  • Pantry and fridge: what you buy you carry with you until you put it away in the kitchen of a house of yours. Fresh food goes in the fridge and the rest in the pantry, each house with its own and with limited space.
  • Fresh food goes off: the game says how long each thing has left, warns you about what expires today or tomorrow, and expired food is thrown away by itself, saying so.
  • What is eaten as it is, like a piece of fruit, a sandwich or a bottle of water, you can carry with you and eat on the street, from the phone, in Inventory.
  • Cooking at home: walk through your house to the kitchen and choose a recipe. There are eleven, from the tuna sandwich to the seafood paella. Each one uses its ingredients and takes its time. Home-cooked food is much more filling than packaged food and cheaper than eating out; you get several servings, and the ones you don't eat are kept in the fridge.
  • Sometimes a dish turns out first-rate and sometimes it gets a bit burnt. Your skill in the kitchen improves with practice, and an equipped kitchen helps.
  • Home delivery food: when you are in a house of yours, the phone lets you order food and drinks. You choose from the menu, wait about half an hour of game time and they ring the doorbell. If you are not at home, the delivery person calls you, waits a while and leaves.
  • None of this is compulsory: without realistic life, eating is for recovering health and mood, and nobody has to cook.

Objective, GPS and chases

  • The O key first says where the objective is: name, meters, direction and street. In a chase, when following someone or with the clock running, one press says only where it is and where it is heading; two quick presses read the whole explanation.
  • Shift O, new: go back to the mission or job objective. It always does the same thing: it drops any detour, marks it on the GPS and, if it is close, leaves it selected on the radar. It is also on the phone, and it is the first entry in the L list of places.
  • On the phone, Available missions starts with the mission you have in progress, with the position of its objective.
  • The GPS has a single destination, which is the one G says, and the directions, the beacon and the autopilot all go to it. What you mark during a mission is a detour: when you finish it, the GPS goes back to the mission and says so.
  • Detours end by themselves: when you get into the vehicle, reach the person, pick up the item or arrive at the place. If what you were looking for disappears, the game also says so and goes back.
  • If the mission changes destination while you are on a detour, the game tells you that you are still on the detour and where the GPS will go afterwards.
  • Control G, new: recovers the last destination or detour you removed with Shift G. With no mission and no destination, G alone does the same.
  • When a chase in a vehicle starts, the game explains that you need a fast vehicle and how to follow whoever is fleeing. The notices about where they are going also say the street and which way they are heading.
  • New option, Chase assist: more frequent notices, every turn called out, the person fleeing slows down sooner if you fall behind, a bit more time when there is a limit, and more leeway when following someone on foot.

Life, reputation and consequences: being good, bad or both

  • Standing with the neighbors and street reputation are still two separate scores, and now both are felt in the world. On the phone, in Life, reputation and consequences, you can read how the city sees you, how the street sees you and what you gain or lose because of it.
  • Where people like you, shops give you discounts and let you buy on credit, neighbors sometimes look the other way and an officer gives you the benefit of the doubt. Where people avoid you, they charge you more and report you more readily.
  • With street reputation, bodyguards charge less, bribes work better and gangs respect your businesses. Without reputation, without a gang and without an escort, some gang may come by asking for money "for protection": you decide whether to pay, report it, refuse or stand up to them.
  • Criminal record: each arrest goes on your record, more serious the more you were wanted. It closes off joining the police, jobs of trust and, if serious, loans. Records expire by themselves and are reduced with community service or with a lawyer. Anything before this version does not count.
  • Permanent contract, at the job centers: a boss with a name and a personality, shifts to complete each week, a salary on top of what you earn per shift, evaluations, warnings and dismissal if you don't show up, and promotions to positions with real responsibility. If you leave on good terms, a letter of recommendation. Without a contract, everything stays as always.
  • Studies with qualifications, at the academies: seven qualifications that take several afternoons and money, and that open up the better positions and the upgrades for your businesses. You can study and work on the same day.
  • Your businesses are managed: upgrades, employees, prices, opening hours and a reputation among the customers that rises if you look after them and falls if you neglect them. Every now and then there is an inspection.
  • The bank lends: personal loans and mortgages to buy a house or a business paying only the down payment, depending on your standing, your income and your record. Installments are charged automatically, with a warning; if you don't pay, a surcharge and, the third time, seizure of part of what you earn, never all at once.
  • Taxes and bills, mild: electricity and water for your houses, their property tax and a tax on what your businesses bring in, every week, with a warning two days before and always available to check. Games already started have fourteen days' grace and a notice explaining it.
  • Doing good: when you are on foot, every so often you come across a lost wallet, an injured person, a robbery, a fight, a lost child... and you decide whether to help, carry on your way or take advantage.
  • The double life has rules: what you are seen doing gets around, and it can end up in the newspaper and cost you your standing and your job. You are protected by discretion, a front business for the money, bribes and a lawyer, each with its price and its risk.
  • The Right Path: a new story in nine chapters, with decisions, temptations and four endings, which starts at the Puerto Bravo Job Center as soon as you have seen your aunt and is completed without committing a single crime. The game says from the start that there are two roads, the street and The Right Path, and that you can follow both. Its scenes have recorded voices, like the others: each new character has their own voice, they call you by your name and address you as male or female; with a character you created, the lines that say your name are generated by the game on your computer.
  • Love life suitable for everyone: without turning on adult content, you can meet someone, go on dates, become a couple, get engaged, live together and get married.
  • Eight new achievements: Clean Record, Model Citizen, Two-Faced, Home with a Name, Complete Race, three qualifications, The Right Path and Good Samaritan.
  • Four new sounds: dismissal, inspection, bill and qualification.
  • On an online server there are permanent contracts, with the salary paid by the server, qualifications, criminal records and, if its owner wants, shop treatment based on your standing. There are no loans, mortgages, taxes or business management, and The Right Path story is played offline; the game tells you if you try.

New activities

  • You can rob any business that has a till, not only shops and banks: repair shops, gyms, gun shops, pharmacies, bars, cinemas, hotels, casinos and the rest. Where there is a guard, you have to deal with them first.
  • The businesses you rob remember you for a few days: either they don't open the door to you, or they call the police as soon as they see you. Shops too.
  • The tavern board offers three different jobs to choose from every day: the usual target, one with an escort that arrives in a vehicle, one who flees by car, one holed up in a business, one you have to follow first, one you only have to teach a lesson, something to recover or someone to protect.
  • Working as a police officer, optional mode: join the force at a police station, start your shift with a patrol car and respond to calls on the radio: robberies, fights, reckless drivers and chases. Order people to stop, subdue, arrest and fine; get paid for each call and get promoted. If you commit a crime on duty, you lose your badge.
  • With adult content, plans as a couple: an aimless walk, the cinema, a dinner, a boat trip, an afternoon at the beach and more, each one told with scenes that change.
  • With adult content, living as a couple, optional: you can ask your partner to move in with you. Each person decides. If they accept, you find them at home, you eat together, they eat what you cook, they ask you for things and they notice if you don't show up. You can ask them to leave.
  • With adult content, suppliers can be robbed to take their merchandise and their cash. It has consequences: they stop selling to you, they raise their prices or they send people after you.
  • "My music", in the pause menu, explains in four steps how to put your songs on the radio and opens the folder for you. The folder can also be opened from Options, Sound and music, and the My music station, when it is empty, explains how to fill it.

Planned heists with your gang

  • Before robbing a bank, a business with a till or a supplier, if you have members of your gang with you, you can plan the heist: in the place's menu, Plan the heist with your gang. It is voluntary: the usual robbery is still in its own option and does not change.
  • Five roles to hand out. The driver waits outside with the engine running, in a vehicle of yours or one of the gang's, and gives you a head start in the getaway. The lookout keeps the police busy and keeps telling you how long until they arrive. The escort goes in with you and stops anyone from playing the hero or raising the alarm too early. The safecracker opens the safe faster, or alone while you cover them. The shooter fires from the vehicle at whoever is following you.
  • You also choose your own part: opening the safe or covering whoever opens it, and driving or riding as a passenger.
  • The heist has five phases that the game announces out loud: entry, control, safe, exit and getaway. The O key tells you which one you are in and who is doing what.
  • In a planned heist the safe takes time to open: you have to stay next to it, you hear how it progresses, and if you move away, it stops.
  • Each member of your gang now has a name, aim, driving, nerves and loyalty, different in each game. They perform according to what they are worth, and with each heist that goes well they learn and become more loyal to you. You can see it on the phone, in Territories, Your gang, Your crew.
  • Things can go wrong, a different way each time: the driver arrives late, the safecracker's hands shake, the escort is wounded and out of action for a few days, or the lookout is arrested and, depending on their loyalty, keeps quiet or gives your name.
  • At the end the loot is split: each one takes their share, and before you start the menu tells you how much you will keep.
  • Explained inside the game, in How this works, and in the manual. On an online server and when playing with friends there are no planned heists; there, the usual robbery remains.

Real flying

  • Helicopters and light planes have altitude. Up Arrow takes off, D climbs, A descends and F lands: the aircraft comes down by itself and, when it touches the ground, you get out. A note sounds when the altitude changes, higher pitched the higher you are.
  • Flying high, the police on the ground lose sight of you. Flying low, everything is as always.
  • The light plane needs the runway of an airport or an airfield, and builds up speed to take off. Anywhere else it is a forced landing, which you have to confirm and which damages it.
  • Aircraft burn fuel, which is refilled at heliports, airports and airfields. The game warns you when you are on reserve; if it runs out, the engine stops and you glide.
  • You go from one town to another on a real crossing: when you fly to the edge of the town, or with Enter at a heliport or an airport, you choose a destination. There is a heading, altitude, different wind on each flight and a control tower that talks to you over the radio.
  • The autopilot does everything if you want, and the crossing can be sped up with N. Enter says how far is left, the heading, the altitude, the wind and the fuel.
  • Things happen in the air: turbulence, another aircraft you have to give way to, and storms you can go around, fly over or go through. With rain there is more wind and more storms.
  • Flying by hand, two clicks tell you which way to turn. When you arrive, the tower gives you the approach; with assisted landing the aircraft descends and lines up on its own, and without it you do it yourself.
  • Two ways to arrive: land at the airfield, or fly in low over the town and set down wherever you want.
  • Hangars at heliports, airports and airfields: they are rented by the day or bought. Aircraft kept there are not lost, they are refueled and repaired there, and they appear on the phone, in My vehicles, from where they can also be sent to the hangar.
  • Hijacking an airliner, with a weapon, at any airfield. It is a major crime: high wanted level, a serious criminal record and a radar that follows you while you fly high. If you land at an airfield with the radar on you, they are waiting for you. Flying low, it loses you.
  • A different job every day at each airfield: someone pays for the hijacked aircraft, delivered to a discreet spot in another town and without the radar on you.
  • The honest road: the commercial pilot license, which you get at the academy, opens up passenger and cargo flights between towns. Three jobs a day at each airfield, with your own aircraft or one of the company's.
  • Explained inside the game, in How flying works, in the menu of any heliport or airport.
  • On an online server you fly the same way and there are hangars; there are no hijackings or commercial flights.
  • With real flight turned off in Options, everything is as before.
